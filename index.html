What I do with your app.

  1. 1

    I take a deep look.

    I read your code and use your app the way your customers do, to find problems in the code, the app and the product.

  2. 2

    I try to break it.

    I try what your users might do, and a few things they shouldn't.

  3. 3

    I sort what I find.

    Every finding gets a severity, so you know what matters most.

    • CriticalSomeone can see data or take money they shouldn't.
    • HighIt breaks things for your customers, or will soon.
    • MediumIt slows you down or annoys your users. Nothing is at risk.
    • LowWorth fixing when you're in there anyway.

    Example findingCriticalYour customers' data

    Any logged-in customer can see every other customer's orders.

    How to see it yourself

    1. Log in as any customer.
    2. Open one of your orders.
    3. Change the number at the end of the web address.

    Ways to fix it

    Before your server sends an order, check that it belongs to the person asking. Then add a test, so it stays fixed.

    For your developer

    The order endpoint returns any order by its ID without checking who owns it. Add the ownership check in the handler, or a row-level security policy if you use Supabase, and cover it with an end-to-end test.

  4. 4

    You get a plan.

    I put every finding in order: what to fix now, what next, and what can wait. We go through it together on a call.

    Fix now

    • Customers can see each other's orders
    • Prices can be changed in the browser
    • Your OpenAI key is public

    Fix next

    • No backups
    • No limit on sign-ups

    Later

    • New users see an empty dashboard
    • Search gets slow with many customers

Questions before you write.

Will you tell me to rewrite my app?

It depends. If the findings are really bad, near impossible to untangle and too risky for production, then yes. In that case I plan the rebuild with you: how to set up your AI tools and the way you work with them, so the new version is robust from the start.

I'm not technical. Can I still work with you?

Yes. Every finding says what's wrong and why it matters in plain words, with steps you can follow to see it yourself. The technical details are there for whoever fixes it.

Which technologies do you work with?

Any stack, as long as it's JavaScript or TypeScript.

Is this a security audit?

No, it's not a certified security test. But if I find security problems, they're in the report.

What happens to my code?

I'll sign an NDA if you want one. When we're done, everything that's yours is handed over to you.

How long does it take?

A quick review takes a few days. A deep review takes days to weeks. Fixing things and working together can run for weeks or months.

Can you fix the problems too?

Yes, with you or for you, and you get a price for the fixes before I start.
