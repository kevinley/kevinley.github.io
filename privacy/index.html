Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other sites. Only two things involve your personal data: the IP address the host logs when it serves a page, and what you send me by email.

When you visit this site

GitHub Pages hosts this site. It is run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, United States. When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. Its servers may also record technical details of the request, such as the time, the page asked for and your browser. I can't see these logs.

  • Legal basis: my legitimate interest in delivering the site securely and reliably (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Transfer to the United States: GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognised as adequate on 10 July 2023 (Article 45 GDPR). GitHub also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Article 46 GDPR).
  • How long: GitHub decides how long it keeps its logs. Its privacy statement explains it.

Everything else on the page runs only in your browser: the fonts and pictures come from this site, and your answers to the ten questions about your app are counted in your browser and never sent anywhere. The count only reaches me if you leave it in your email.

When you email me

I receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you write, such as the link to your app or your count from the ten questions. I use it to answer you and, if we decide to work together, to prepare and run the project.

  • Legal basis: steps you ask for before a contract, and the contract itself (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). For anything else, my legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Email provider: my mailbox is iCloud Mail, provided in the EU by Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland.
  • How long: as long as I need it to answer you and, if we work together, for the project. Quotes, invoices and the records behind them are kept for ten years, because Luxembourg law requires it (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

I don't sell your data and don't share it with anyone other than the services named here, unless the law requires it. You don't have to give me any data, but without an email address I can't answer you. I make no automated decisions about you and build no profiles.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time:

  • which of your data I hold, and for a copy of it (Article 15 GDPR);
  • to correct it (Article 16) or delete it (Article 17);
  • to limit what I do with it (Article 18);
  • for the data you gave me, in a common file format, so you can take it elsewhere (Article 20);
  • to stop using it where I rely on my legitimate interest (Article 21).

Email me and I'll answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Luxembourg that is the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 15, Boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux, cnpd.public.lu. You can also complain to the authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes

If the site starts using another service, this notice changes first, and the date at the top shows when.
